A fast, lightweight Android application designed to help students and guardians in Bangladesh quickly check public examination results (NATIONAL UNIVERSITY, SSC, HSC including Dakhil, Alim, and Technical boards) and calculate academic performance (GPA/CGPA).

---

## ✨ Features

This application offers essential tools for academic management and result retrieval:

- **Direct Result Viewing:** Provides one-click access to official government and educational board result publication websites.
  - Covers all major boards: Dhaka, Rajshahi, Comilla, Jessore, Chittagong, Barisal, Sylhet, Dinajpur, Mymensingh.
  - Includes Madrasah and Technical Education Boards.
  - Dedicated links for National University (Recently Published, Archive, Admission Results).

- **Offline GPA/CGPA Calculator:** Quickly calculate your Grade Point Average (GPA) or Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) without needing an internet connection. All data is processed locally on your device.

- **Result via SMS:** Conveniently initiates the official SMS format to retrieve results, pre-filling the required details for the user's convenience (standard carrier charges apply).

- **Local Storage Saving:** Allows users to save a screenshot or image of their viewed results locally on their device.
